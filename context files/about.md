Public Transit lost and found
Activity A - Problem Statement
Passengers who lose belongings on public transit often have difficulty determining whether their items have been recovered and getting them back. Transit employees and lost-and-found staff must also handle recovered items and connect them with the correct owners, often with incomplete or inconsistent information. This can make the recovery process time-consuming and can result in delays, unsuccessful recoveries, or incorrect claims.

Pitch
Our project is about the problem of lost property on public transit.
When a passenger loses something on a bus or train, they may know roughly where and when they lost it, but they usually don't know whether the item has been found. At the same time, transit employees and lost-and-found staff may be handling many recovered items and have to determine which lost reports, if any, correspond to those items.
The problem is that information about lost and recovered property can be incomplete or difficult to connect. This can lead to passengers having difficulty recovering their belongings, while staff spend significant time searching and verifying claims.
Our main stakeholders are passengers, transit employees, and lost-and-found staff. They have different concerns: passengers want their property returned, employees want reporting to be quick and practical, and lost-and-found staff need to accurately identify and verify claims.
For our scope, we are focusing on managing information about lost and recovered items and the recovery process. We are not trying to operate the transit system, physically transport items, or conduct police investigations.

Activity B - Scope Boundary
Inside the system boundary
Recording lost-item reports
Information about what was lost, when/where it was lost, and a description.
Recording recovered items
Information about items found by transit employees or other authorized personnel.
Managing the recovery process
Connecting lost reports with recovered items and managing the process of returning an item to its owner.
Claim and ownership verification
Supporting staff in determining whether a person can legitimately claim a recovered item.
transporting items through Delivery Partner
The system also includes delivery of the object to the verified owner.

Outside the system boundary
Operating public transit
Bus/train operations and vehicle scheduling are outside the system.
Police investigations
Theft investigations or determining criminal responsibility are outside the system.
Determining legal ownership
The system does not make legal judgments about ownership in disputed cases.
Activity C - Stakeholder Richness Check
Stakeholder
Main concern/objective
Passenger
Wants to recover their lost property quickly and receive useful information about its status.
Transit employee / operator
Wants to report recovered items efficiently without adding significant work to their normal duties.
Lost-and-found staff
Wants to accurately match lost reports with recovered items and prevent incorrect claims.
Transit management
Wants the process to be efficient, consistent, auditable, and appropriately controlled.
