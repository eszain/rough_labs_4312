## Scoring policy
This lab is scored out of 10 raw marks and scaled to a nominal 1% course value if it is among the student's best 10 of 12 labs. The checkpoint artifact is normally a team artifact, but individual attendance/participation is required for credit; an absent student does not automatically inherit the team's lab mark. Approved accommodations are handled according to course procedures.

## Rubric

| Criterion | Max | Full-credit evidence | Partial-credit evidence | Minimal/no-credit evidence |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Participation observed elicitation** | 2 | Substantive participation in interviewer/stakeholder/observer role. | Participation present but limited. | Little substantive participation. |
| **Question quality** | 2 | Mostly neutral, open, purposeful questions with effective follow-ups. | Some leading/closed or unfocused questions. | Questions strongly bias or fail to probe the problem. |
| **Notes and synthesis** | 2 | Findings capture needs, exceptions, terminology, and contradictions. | Basic notes with limited synthesis. | Sparse or superficial notes. |
| **Evidence coding** | 2 | Simulation statements, inferences, and assumptions are clearly distinguished. | Minor source/confidence confusion. | Simulated/inferred claims presented as established facts. |
| **Follow-up questions** | 2 | At least five strong questions, including exception/failure probes. | Questions present but mostly routine. | Few or weak follow-ups. |
