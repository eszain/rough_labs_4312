# EECS4312 Lab 4: Simulated Requirements Elicitation

**Course:** EECS4312 Software Requirements Engineering | York University - Fall 2026  
**Date & Time:** Thursday, October 1, 2026 | 4:00-5:00 PM  
**Project connection:** Sprint 1 elicitation evidence  
**Lab grading:** 10 raw marks, scaled to nominal 1%  

## Learning objectives
* Plan and conduct a structured elicitation conversation.
* Use neutral follow-up questions rather than leading the stakeholder.
* Distinguish elicited statements from analyst inference and simulation assumptions.

## Before the lab
* Bring a short elicitation goal and 8-10 draft questions.
* Review the course rule that simulated evidence must be labeled as simulation.

## In-lab schedule

| Time | Activity |
| :--- | :--- |
| 0-8 min | TA briefing and role assignment. |
| 8-20 min | Interview round 1. |

## Activities

### Activity A - Interview
Use open questions first, then targeted follow-ups. Look for tacit knowledge, exceptions, domain terms, workarounds, and contradictions. Do not argue with the stakeholder or sell your preferred design.

### Activity B - Evidence coding
From the conversation, record at least five findings. For each, note whether it is a direct statement in the simulation, an analyst inference, or an unresolved assumption. If you later use the material in the project, label its source as simulation; do not present it as real-user evidence.

### Activity C - Follow-up strategy
Write at least five follow-up questions that would reduce uncertainty. At least two should probe exceptions/failure cases rather than normal workflow.

## Lab checkpoint / deliverable
Commit `labs/lab04/elicitation-exercise.md` containing the elicitation objective, question set, concise notes, five coded findings, two contradictions/ambiguities if found, and five follow-up questions.
