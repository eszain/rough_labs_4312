### Learning Objectives

- Refine stakeholder/business goals into operational requirements.
- Identify obstacles and conflicts that threaten goal satisfaction.
- Convert vague quality goals into measurable or objectively assessable requirements.

---

### Before the Lab

- Bring 2–3 important project goals and current quality requirements.
- Bring one requirement whose rationale is currently weak or unclear.

### Activities

#### Activity A – Goal Model
Create/refine a small goal model with at least one decomposition and one alternative. Link at least three operational goals to requirements.

#### Activity B – Obstacles
Identify at least two meaningful obstacles or conflict conditions. State the affected goal/requirement and one mitigation, refinement, or trade-off question.

#### Activity C – Quality Requirements
Rewrite at least three vague quality statements. Each should specify relevant operating conditions, observable response/quality, and a defensible threshold/criterion where possible. If a numeric threshold is provisional, label it as an assumption rather than pretending it came from evidence.
