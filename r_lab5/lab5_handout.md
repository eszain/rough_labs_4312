Learning Objectives 

* Turn a stakeholder goal into a precise scenario/use case.


* Represent alternative and exception flows, not only the happy path.


* Write acceptance criteria that can actually determine pass/fail.



---

Before the Lab 

* Bring one important stakeholder goal and related candidate functional requirements.


* Bring the current glossary so terminology can be checked.

---

Activities 

* 
**Activity A: Scenario/use case:** Create one detailed use case with actor, goal, trigger, preconditions, main success flow, at least two alternative/exception paths, and postconditions.


* 
**Activity B: Acceptance criteria:** Write at least five acceptance criteria. At least one must address an exception/failure case and at least one must include a measurable threshold or objectively observable result where appropriate.


* 
**Activity C: Peer falsification test:** Another team tries to find an implementation that would technically satisfy your wording while violating the intent. Revise criteria that are too vague, underspecified, or solution-biased.
