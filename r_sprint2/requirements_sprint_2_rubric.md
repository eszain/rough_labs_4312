### Marking Philosophy

Sprint 2 evaluates requirements analysis and engineering judgment. Marks should not be awarded merely because a document, diagram, table, or GitHub label exists.

Evidence of strong work includes:
* Meaningful relationships among artifacts;
* Explicit rationale;
* Defensible assumptions;
* Measurable quality requirements;
* Analysis that leads to requirement changes;
* Visible preservation of requirements history;
* Internally consistent decisions.

*Where the rubric gives quantitative guidelines, do not reward artificial inflation. A smaller set of meaningful goals, requirements, or dependencies may be stronger than a large set of superficial ones.*
