## Learning Objectives

* Apply a transparent prioritization method rather than intuitive ranking alone.
* Recognize sensitivity to weights/criteria.
* Negotiate a requirements-scope decision while preserving stakeholder rationale.

---

## Before the Lab

* Bring 10–12 accepted requirements with current priorities.
* Identify at least two stakeholder perspectives that could reasonably disagree.

## Activities

### Activity A: Prioritization
Use a transparent method such as weighted scoring, pairwise comparison, or cost-value reasoning for at least eight requirements. Define your criteria and scales.

### Activity B: Sensitivity
Change one important weighting/assumption. Record whether the ranking is robust or fragile and why that matters.

### Activity C: Negotiation
Assign stakeholder roles within the team. Negotiate one realistic conflict or constrained release/scope decision. Record stakeholder goals, preferred outcomes, concessions, alternatives, and rationale. A simple majority vote is not enough by itself.
