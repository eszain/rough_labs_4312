### Activities

#### Activity A – Five-requirement sample
Choose a business/stakeholder requirement, two functional requirements, one preliminary quality requirement, and one constraint/assumption-sensitive item. Check atomicity, clarity, rationale, source, priority, acceptance/verification idea, and GitHub Issue state.

#### Activity B – Trace chains
Build at least three chains of the form `evidence/goal -> requirement -> scenario/use case -> acceptance criterion`. Identify any broken or weak link.

#### Activity C – Model interrogation
Ask what your model reveals that the prose alone did not. Record at least one missing requirement, ambiguity, terminology inconsistency, or model limitation discovered during the review.

#### Activity D – Mini walkthrough
Show the TA a small, representative portion of the baseline. The purpose is rapid feedback, not a live project presentation grade.
