# EECS4312: Software Requirements Engineering

## Lab 9: Cross-Team Requirements Inspection

**York University – Fall 2026**

* **Date:** Thursday, November 12, 2026 (4:00–5:00 PM)

* **Project Connection:** Sprint 3 Phase A

* **Lab Grading:** 10 raw marks, scaled to nominal 1%

## Learning Objectives

* Perform a structured inspection of another team's requirements rather than giving general feedback.

* Classify defects by type and severity.

* Provide actionable evidence that the receiving team can accept, reject, defer, or clarify.

## Before the Lab

* Prepare the peer-inspection packet specified by the instructor/TA (normally 10–15 representative requirements, one model, and selected traceability).

* Do not include unnecessary personal/sensitive information.

## In-Lab Schedule

| Time | Activity |
| :--- | :--- |
| **0–8 min** | TA assigns/explains review pairs and defect taxonomy. |
| **8–32 min** | Independent/paired inspection of the other team packet. |
| **32–42 min** | Consolidate findings and severity. |
| **42–52 min** | Reviewer/author discussion: clarify findings without negotiating away valid defects. |
| **52–60 min** | Submit report and TA sign-off. |

## Activities

Inspect for ambiguity, omission, inconsistency, contradiction, non-atomic wording, unverifiability, hidden assumptions, duplication, premature design, missing source/rationale, broken traceability, and model/text mismatch.

Record at least five substantive findings where warranted. Quality matters more than quantity; do not manufacture minor defects merely to reach a number. Each finding should include location/ID, defect type, severity, explanation, and a concrete clarification question or correction suggestion.

## Lab Checkpoint / Deliverable

Submit/commit the review in the mechanism specified by the TA as `labs/lab09/peer-inspection-report.md`.

The receiving team should preserve the report as Sprint 3 validation/inspection evidence and later disposition each significant finding.

### TA Sign-off

TA checks that findings are specific, technically relevant, and supported by the inspected artifact.

Lab activities are designed to be completed primarily during the scheduled hour. Unless the TA states otherwise, the concise checkpoint artifact should be committed/submitted by **6:00 PM** on the lab day.

The lab grade is separate from the semester-project grade even when the artifact later becomes project evidence.
